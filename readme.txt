== Description ==

[Woorise](https://woorise.com/) is a modern form builder that helps businesses capture leads, collect feedback, and engage their audience with interactive forms, surveys, quizzes, giveaways, and automated workflows. 

The plugin makes it easy to embed any Woorise campaign — such as a form, survey, quiz, giveaway, or popup — directly into your WordPress site. A [free Woorise account](https://woorise.com/signup) is required to use the plugin.

### How to add a Woorise campaign to your website

Woorise includes a dedicated block that lets you easily embed any form, quiz, survey, or popup on any WordPress post or page. Just copy the Embed ID from your Woorise dashboard and paste it into the Woorise block.

If you want to display a campaign across multiple pages using conditional rules, you can set this up by creating a new Embed from the Woorise menu in your WordPress admin area.

For more details, including advanced placements and setup options, check out our full guide:
[https://woorise.com/help/wordpress](https://woorise.com/help/wordpress)

== Installation ==

1. Install the plugin through the WordPress plugins screen or upload the plugin files to the /wp-content/plugins/ directory.
1. Activate the plugin through the Plugins screen in WordPress.
1. Copy your campaign’s Embed ID and paste it into the Woorise block, or create a new Embed in your WordPress dashboard to display it conditionally across your site.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= What types of campaigns can I build with Woorise? =

Woorise allows you to create a wide variety of high-converting campaigns such as forms, surveys, quizzes, popups, and viral giveaways. Whether your goal is to collect leads, accept payments, engage audiences, or grow your social media following, Woorise gives you all the tools to build interactive and engaging experiences without any coding.

= What does the plugin do? =

The Woorise WordPress plugin allows you to easily embed any Woorise campaign on your WordPress site. You can display a campaign on a single post or page using the Woorise block or shortcode, or use Display Conditions to show it across multiple pages or site-wide.

= What is Woorise? =

Woorise is a modern form builder with automated workflows that helps you capture leads, collect feedback and qualify customers. Turn your forms into branded, interactive experiences with quizzes, surveys, giveaways, personalized outcomes and payments—all without writing code.

= Do I need a Woorise account to use this plugin? =

Yes. A Woorise account is required. You can get started with a free account. See [Woorise plans](https://woorise.com/pricing) for current features and limits.

= Is the plugin free to use? =

Yes, the plugin is completely free to install and use. You’ll need a Woorise account, and you can get started with our free plan. Paid plans are available for additional features and higher limits.

= Does the plugin slow down my site? =

The plugin loads campaign content from Woorise. Loading times depend on your campaign, WordPress theme, other plugins, and network connection.

= Does the plugin work with the block editor and page builders like Elementor? =

Yes! Woorise works smoothly with all major WordPress page builders including Elementor, Divi, Beaver Builder, and others. You can easily add a campaign using the Woorise block or shortcode anywhere on your site.

= Can I use Woorise without writing any code? =

Absolutely. All campaign creation and customizations are handled in the Woorise dashboard using an intuitive drag & drop interface. You never need to touch code to build, update, or embed your campaigns.

= How do I style my embedded campaign to match my theme? =

All styling and design customizations happen directly inside your Woorise dashboard. Any changes you make are instantly reflected on your website in real time — without the need to edit any WordPress templates or code.

= Can I track form or campaign submissions inside WordPress? =

Campaign entries are stored in Woorise. You can view entries, analytics, and reports in your Woorise dashboard.
